  • Move — WASD on desktop, or the virtual joystick (touch the lower-left) on mobile.
  • Attack — all weapons fire automatically; just position yourself.
  • XP & Leveling — enemies drop XP gems. Collect them to level up and pick 1 of 3 upgrades.
  • Reroll — unhappy with your 3 choices? Use a reroll to draw a new set.
  • Weapons & Passives — you can carry up to 4 weapons and 4 passives (buy extra slots in the shop or from the trader).
  • Evolutions — a maxed weapon + a specific passive = an evolution. They appear as a special card.
  • Speed — F = 1x, G = 2x, H = 4x (or tap the 1x/2x/4x buttons). Speed affects everything, including how fast danger ramps up.
  • Difficulty Map — hold Z (or tap the 🗺️ button) to see zones: green = easy, yellow = medium, red = hard. Harder zones spawn tougher enemies and better rewards.
  • Shrine — walk up to a shrine to buy blessings (damage, speed, HP, crit, pickup). Cost doubles each use.
  • Trader — a roaming trader sells rerolls, deletes, heals, extra slots, temporary buffs, and weapon masteries. He restocks over time.
  • Gold — earn gold from kills, bosses, and chests; spend it in the shop, shrine, and trader. Gold persists between runs.
  • Bosses — 4 story bosses await (Warden, Tyrant, Leviathan, Overlord), each with unique attacks and special abilities. Defeat the Overlord around 20 minutes.
  • Endless Mode — after the Overlord, endless bosses keep spawning so you can chase a high score.
  • Respawn — if you die, you can spend gold to revive (up to twice per run).
  • Masteries — after evolving a weapon, the trader offers run-long masteries that further boost it.
  • Chests — bosses drop chests; open them to spin for free upgrades.
  • Elites & Modifiers — orange elites have modifiers (armored, swift, berserk, regenerating, split, vampiric) that change how they fight.
  • Challenges — check the Achievements screen for Insane challenges with special rules and difficulty tiers.
